Good to know

Before ordering

Can I request a design I don't see online?

Yes. Custom work is a major part of the site. Use the custom-order form to describe what you have in mind.

Will every item always be in stock?

No. Handmade inventory naturally changes. Products can be marked Available, Made to Order, Sold, or Coming Soon.

Can I send an inspiration picture?

Yes. The custom-order flow is designed to accept an inspiration image along with your colors, wording, size, and other details.

How will payment work?

The finished site can connect to a secure checkout provider. Prices and payment setup can be added after the business finalizes its pricing structure.
